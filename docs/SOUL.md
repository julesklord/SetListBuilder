# Project Soul

## Vision
To remove the cognitive load of setlist planning for live performers, allowing them to focus entirely on their musical expression while ensuring a balanced and professional show.

## Principles
1. **Reliability is Victory:** A tool used in live environments must never fail. No backend dependencies, no cloud fragility.
2. **Speed as a Feature:** Generations, searches, and UI interactions must be instantaneous (<100ms).
3. **Data Sovereignty:** The user's repertoire and shows belong to them. Local-only storage is a non-negotiable privacy feature.
4. **Intelligent Balance:** Music is both energy and physical effort. The tool must respect both the audience's arc and the musicians' stamina.
5. **Universal Access:** Provide a high-density, professional experience that works on any screen size.

## Values
- **Integrity:** Explicit and auditable logic in song selection and balancing.
- **Craftsmanship:** Clean, performant, and well-documented vanilla JS code.
- **Empowerment:** Giving bands the tools to "doctor" their own setlist health.
- **Inclusivity:** Built-in multi-language support for a global community of musicians.
